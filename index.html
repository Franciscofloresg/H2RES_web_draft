About H2RES

Integrated Energy System Modelling

Overview

H2RES is an open-source modeling framework that provides a transparent model to explore energy transition pathways by integrating power systems, storage, hydrogen technologies, and demand flexibility within an integrated optimisation structure.

Development History

H2RES has evolved through two decades of peer-reviewed research, progressing from early island-based renewable energy studies to a comprehensive modeling framework addressing sector coupling, flexibility, and long-term decarbonisation pathways across diverse energy systems.

Institutions & Collaborations

H2RES is supported by universities and research partners working together to collaborate and share knowledge in open energy system modeling.

Governance & Licensing

H2RES follows an open governance approach and is distributed under an open-source license, supporting transparent development and responsible use of the model.

Summer School on Energy Planning of 100% Renewable Energy Systems

Summer school on energy planning of 100% renewable energy systems is centered on energy and climate planning and modelling of smart energy systems and exchange of experiences with experts in the field. A special focus will be on the improvements of existing energy planning tool and models, with improved data management, policy, and scenario assessment, as well as systematic dynamic modelling of relevant environmental, economic, social, and technological variables. Finding a new and improved representation of smart energy systems is a key issue in calculating policy scenarios and their emissions.

H2RES Competition

Modellers that submit the model of their national energy system that is:

Eligibility Criteria

  • 100 % RES in total energy supply
  • Cheaper than the scenario based on fossil fuels
  • No more than 30 % of final energy supplied by biomass
  • Respects the limitations on installation of VRES capacities
  • Possesses no more than 48 hours worth of electricity storage in a form of battery stationary storage

Get Started

Set Up H2RES for Your First Run

Requirements and Environment

+
  • Install Python in a local or Anaconda environment.
  • Install Gurobi and activate a valid license.
  • Use Spyder, VS Code, or another Python editor to run the model.
$ python --version
$ gurobi_cl --version

Install Dependencies and Activate Gurobi

+
  • Install the required Python dependencies.
  • Request a Gurobi license if you do not already have one.
  • Academic users can typically use the named-user academic license.
$ pip install gurobipy pandas numpy matplotlib
Register for Gurobi Official Install Guide

Download the Model and Run a Baseline Case

+
  • Download the model files and extract them into a working directory.
  • Open main.py and start from the baseline configuration.
  • Review the version resources before adapting the model to a new case study.
$ python main.py

Documentation

General Guidance and Version-Specific Resources

Overview and Scope

H2RES is an open-source framework for integrated energy system planning, designed to evaluate long-term transition pathways with hourly system representation, capacity expansion, system operation, sector coupling, storage, hydrogen, and power-to-X.

Open baseline version resources →

Workflow

The general workflow covers data preparation, scenario definition, model execution, and interpretation of results. The baseline setup begins from main.py and can then be adapted for new studies, assumptions, and case-specific datasets.

Review setup workflow →

Inputs and Outputs

Core inputs include demand, technology, policy, economic, and resource data. Core outputs include dispatch, capacity expansion, reserve behavior, total system costs, and emissions indicators used for scenario comparison and planning assessment.

See baseline model details →

Version-Specific Resources

General guidance is maintained online, while methodological details remain attached to each model version through version-specific PDFs. Use the Model Versions section to access the relevant user manual and mathematical formulation for each release.
